# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. In this privacy policy, we explain how **Luo BV** (hereinafter "Luo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you make use of the **Helios EMS controller**, the associated cloud platform, the mobile application, and our website.

Helios is an energy management system that can monitor and automatically control connected energy assets, such as solar panels, home batteries, and charging stations, to optimize energy consumption and energy costs.

## 2. What Personal Data Do We Process?

We always limit the processing to data necessary for the functioning of the Helios system and providing our services:

* **Identification and contact details:** Name, email address, and phone number of the user. Needed for account management, system notifications, and technical support.
* **Installation and location data:** The address where the Helios controller is installed. This is necessary for, among other things, relevant weather forecasts and energy market data.
* **Device and network data:** Serial number, MAC address, IP address, firmware version, and system logs. Necessary for identification, security, and correct operation.
* **Energy data:** EAN number, real-time energy production and consumption data, energy tariffs/price structures, and technical configuration of connected assets (solar panels, batteries, charging stations).
* **Financial data (only for business partners):** We process invoicing data and payment history of our direct clients (installers and distributors). Luo does **not** process direct payments or bank details of end users; the financial handling of licenses or hardware takes place between the end user and the respective installer/partner.
* **Diagnostic and operational data:** Error messages, crash logs, and system status information for technical support and remote fault detection.

## 3. Why Do We Process Your Data and On What Basis?

We process your data exclusively based on the following legal grounds:

1. **Performance of a contract:** For monitoring and optimizing energy flows, controlling assets, offering the app/portal, and providing technical support (including software updates).
2. **Legitimate interest:** For the security and stability of our systems, analysis of usage, and the further development of our algorithms and energy models.
3. **Legal obligation:** To comply with accounting, fiscal, or consumer protection obligations towards our direct contracting parties.
4. **Consent:** For marketing communication or newsletters (can be withdrawn at any time).

## 4. With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Data?

Luo never sells your personal data to third parties. We only share data if necessary:

* **Installers and Resellers:** Because Helios systems are delivered and invoiced via independent installers, they have access to technical data for installation, configuration, and first-line support. For the privacy rules regarding your payments to the installer, we refer to the privacy policy of the respective partner.
* **Energy Market Parties:** If necessary for flexibility or grid balancing (e.g., energy provider, Fluvius, Elia, or BRP).
* **Technology Partners:** External providers for cloud hosting, IT infrastructure, or weather data. When processing outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate protection measures.

## 5. Automated Systems and Device Control

Helios uses automated algorithms to optimize energy flows. The system analyzes data (production, consumption, weather, prices) to automatically make decisions regarding the control of connected devices. The objective is to increase efficiency, lower costs, and support the electricity grid.

## 6. Software Updates

Luo can perform remote "Over-the-Air" (OTA) updates to ensure security, stability, and functionality. In doing so, technical system data is processed to guarantee a correct installation.

## 7. How Long Do We Retain Your Data?

* **Account data:** As long as the account is active or up to 10 years after termination of the business relationship (legal retention obligation for invoicing to partners).
* **Technical data:** As long as necessary for the functioning of the service.
* **Anonymization:** After termination, data can be anonymized. This non-traceable data is used indefinitely for statistics and product improvement.

## 8. Security

 We apply strict industry standards, including encryption of data traffic, secure network connections, and active monitoring of our infrastructure to protect your data.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability. Contact us at **info@luo.be** to exercise these rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Authority.

## 10. Cookies

We use cookies for the operation of our platforms and for usage statistics. You can manage your preferences upon your first visit or through your browser settings.

## 11. Amendments
